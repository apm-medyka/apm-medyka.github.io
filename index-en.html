Privacy

APM follows a Local-First approach — your data, including schedules, invoices and business information, stays on your device. No telemetry, advertising servers or analytics tracking. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

Integrations

  • 🏛️ KSeF 2.0 — direct communication with the official Polish Ministry of Finance system
  • 📋 Official ZUS contribution rates and Polish business registry data (public data)
  • ☁️ Optional Google Drive backup using the app's private, hidden application-data folder

Custom Healthcare Facility Templates

By default, invoices and working-hours reports use a neutral Universal template. If your healthcare facility uses its own document layouts or branding, APM can use a custom branded template assigned individually to that organization without affecting templates used for other organizations. Contact us if you would like a custom template for your facility.
