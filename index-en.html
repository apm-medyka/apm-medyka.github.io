Asystent Praktyki Medycznej (APM)

A work organizer for healthcare professionals — useful regardless of your employment type: shift schedules, leave tracking, hours reports, a drug reference database and medical calculators. For self-employed professionals: invoicing for hours or services, with export to KSeF 2.0. Your data stays on your device by default.

Who is APM for?

  • 👨‍⚕️ Doctors
  • 👩‍⚕️ Nurses
  • 🚑 Paramedics
  • 🩺 Physiotherapists
  • 🔬 Medical technicians
  • 🏥 Working across multiple facilities

Privacy

APM follows a Local-First approach — your data, including schedules, invoices and business information, stays on your device by default, and only leaves it through network features you consciously trigger. No telemetry, advertising servers or analytics tracking. For details, see our Privacy Policy.

Integrations

  • 🏛️ KSeF 2.0 — direct communication with the official Polish Ministry of Finance system
  • 📋 Official ZUS contribution rates and Polish business registry data (public data)
  • ☁️ Optional Google Drive backup using the app's private, hidden application-data folder

Custom Healthcare Facility Templates

By default, invoices and working-hours reports use a neutral Universal template. If your healthcare facility uses its own document layouts or branding, APM can use a custom branded template assigned individually to that organization without affecting templates used for other organizations.
